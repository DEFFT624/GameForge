Try the C# foundations at your own pace. This is a test of the teaching and website, not a timed test of you. Take breaks between modules.

1. Your first test

Start with First steps if you are new to coding. Read the goal, follow the example, and predict a small change before answering. Pass the quiz and code blank to finish a lesson; the labs and local RPG are optional.

Try switching lessons and modules, refreshing an unfinished answer, and returning after a break. Your work should stay in the same browser and website address.

2. Note where you get stuck

Record the lesson link, the first unclear word or instruction, what you expected, and what actually happened. For a website bug, include the clicks that reproduce it and your browser or screen size.

If an answer seems wrong, copy the small example and the answer you expected. Avoid names or private information. You can share early feedback before completing the course.

3. Review the full course

After both exercises in all 39 lessons are complete, the course review opens in that browser. It includes prompts about clarity, object design, saving, testing, and navigation. Notes stay locally; copy the report to share it.

Testing with your friend later
  1. Use a hosted preview link after your initial test. A 127.0.0.1 or localhost link points to the computer opening it, so your friend cannot use your local preview from their computer.
  2. Let your friend start in their own browser so their progress is separate. During the call, ask them to think aloud and describe what they expect before clicking.
  3. Give them time to use examples and hints before supplying an answer. Record where help was needed. Stop or take a break whenever they want.
  4. Collect early observations during the call. If they finish the whole course, use its unlocked review in the same browser and copy the report.
  5. Review incorrect teaching, lost work, and access problems first; then unclear explanations and visual improvements. Agree on changes before moving into the next learning path.

No uploads, recordings, or automatic feedback submission. C# examples are displayed as text. Running them is optional and happens in your own console project.
